What we do

Four areas, one view of the whole system

They are usually treated as four separate problems owned by four separate people. They are one system, and most of the expensive failures happen where they meet.

Cloud Operations

Infrastructure that is reliable, observable and boring in the best sense.

  • Architecture and reliability review
  • Monitoring and alerting people trust
  • Infrastructure as code and deployment pipelines

Security

Controls that keep working after the audit, because the threats keep arriving after the code does.

  • Security posture assessment
  • Continuous dependency and vulnerability management
  • Access, secrets and least-privilege review

FinOps

Knowing what you spend, why, and what it buys, including on AI.

  • Cloud and AI cost review
  • Cost attribution by team, product and feature
  • Spend alerts that fire before the invoice does

AI Enablement

Putting AI to work in engineering teams, with the guardrails that make it safe to rely on.

  • AI coding adoption for engineering teams
  • Quality gates for code a person did not type
  • Evaluating and running AI agents in production

How we work

Short-term support
